== item hb-rk-emberglass-lens
"Emberglass Lens" (rare wondrous item).
It is worn over the eyes. It works only while the holder's sheet marks it as equipped, and gives nothing before.
It needs attunement: only a spellcaster can attune to it. The holder asks for it, never in a fight, and it is done on its next Short Rest; ending it works the same way. A creature is attuned to three items at most, and to one copy of an item. Until the holder is attuned it works as the plain base item at most: no bonus and nothing from its grants.
While it is active, the holder gets what follows (the engine makes these a feature of the holder, as for a feat).
  Changes to rolls and numbers:
  Advantage on Perception checks.
  Resistance to fire damage.
  +1 to Dexterity saving throws.
    It holds only when it is a ranged attack. The engine never applies this: at Dexterity saving throws it cannot tell whether it is a ranged attack, and it skips a modifier it cannot be sure of, without telling anyone.
  +5 to its Hit Point maximum.
    The engine never reads its Hit Point maximum from a feature, so this changes nothing.
  An aura 10 feet out from the holder. The holder, and every creature on the holder's side in range with no Total Cover between, get what is listed below, for as long as the holder is up and not Incapacitated. Two of the same aura do not add up.
    +1 to saving throws.
    Immunity to the Frightened condition: a creature in the aura is not given it.
  Immune to the Blinded condition: the engine never gives the holder that condition.
  Senses: tremorsense 10 feet; a sense the holder already has at a longer range stays as it is.
  Resistance to cold damage: such damage is halved.
  Darkvision: 30 feet added to the range of the holder's own sense of that kind, or the whole range of it for a holder who has none.
It holds up to 4 charges, and starts full.
At dawn (6 in the morning) the engine rolls 1d3 for each dawn that has passed and adds it back, up to 4. That happens when a rest or the DM moves the clock past dawn, to an item that has been used; the clock moving round by round in a fight is not watched.
When a use spends its last charge the engine tells the DM; what that does (an item may crumble or not) is the DM's.
Use 1, "Ward": takes a Bonus Action. In a fight it is done on the user's own turn and spends a Bonus Action. The item must be active, and the user up.
  It spends 1 charge, and is refused when fewer are left.
  Its steps run on the user alone: the engine has no way to aim an item's steps at anyone else.
  The user gets +2 to its Armor Class.
    It ends at the end of its next turn, or when the fight ends.
  It gains 1d6 Temporary Hit Points, plus 0: an item's own steps have no spellcasting modifier to add; they replace what it has only if they are more, and they vanish when the use ends.
Use 2, "Scorch": takes the spell's own casting time (action), not the time the entry names. In a fight it is done on the user's own turn and spends an action (a Magic action). The item must be active, and the user up.
  It spends 1 charge, and is refused when fewer are left.
  It casts "Scorching Ray" at level 2: no slot and no components. The save DC is 14. The attack bonus is the user's own (the Proficiency Bonus with no spellcasting). It is cast as at the lowest caster level, through the usual casting rules, so it can be countered.
  Each charge more than the use's own raises the level by one, up to level 4.
Tier: assist. The engine applies what is listed above as applied by the engine; the DM applies the rest of the description.
Parts of this entry never take effect, as said above: the engine does not apply them and tells nobody.
