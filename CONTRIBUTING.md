## Issues first

Open an issue before you write code. Say what you want to change and why. Work
is agreed on the issue, then built.

## How work is handed out

Work is handed out by issue, in small pull requests, one module at a time.
`AGENTS.md` is the working agreement for everyone who writes code here, people
and coding agents alike. Read it, and `CLAUDE.md`, before you start.

## Content

Only SRD 5.2.1 content goes in the repository. Nothing from other books.
