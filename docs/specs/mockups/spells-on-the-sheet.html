Walk a character up: pick New character, tick spells until the top says All chosen, press Save, then Level up to 2, 3, 4, 5. The number in a box is the level the spell was chosen at; one chosen now shows the level you are making, on a gold box. Tap a row to choose it or take it back; ⓘ shows the scroll card on a tap; on a mouse, resting on a row for 3 seconds shows it too, and on touch a press-and-hold (half a second) does. Tap the magnifying glass in the bar to search: the list filters while the search is open, and the ✕ clears it and puts the full list back where it was. Scroll the list: each header sticks under the count bar and the next one pushes it up. The list never moves when you tap.
